Everything a beginner needs

✍️

Hangul, stroke by stroke

Six step-by-step units take you from consonants and vowels to syllable blocks, compound vowels, tense consonants, and final consonants. Watch the stroke order, then trace each letter yourself.

📖

Words with real examples

Learn everyday words in themed lessons — greetings, numbers, food, prices, family, places, and time. Every word comes with an example sentence you can listen to.

💬

Phrases for real situations

Ten situations, from ordering at a café and asking for directions to checking in at a hotel or visiting a pharmacy. Practice with short role-play conversations.

🔁

Review what you missed

Words you get wrong in quizzes go to your review list automatically, so you can practice them again.

🌏

Explanations in your language

Choose English, Vietnamese, or easy Korean for meanings and explanations. Switch anytime in Settings.

🎯

Study at your own pace

Set a daily goal, keep your streak, and see your progress. Skip units you already know with a short test.
