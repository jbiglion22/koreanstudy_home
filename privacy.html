Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Korean Study app (“the app”) handles information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information, and it does not send or share any information with us or third parties. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics.

Data stored on your device

To save your learning, the app stores the following only on your device: lesson and quiz progress, your review list and saved words, study time, and your settings (such as explanation language, daily goal, and reminder time).

Internet access

The app does not request internet permission and does not send data over the network.

Device backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup. This backup is managed by Google under your account and settings, not by us.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules a notification on your device. Reminders are off by default, and you can turn them off at any time.

Text-to-speech

Audio is played with the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The app does not send the spoken text anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete all app data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@example.com.
